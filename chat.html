DharmaAI

Your dharma guide is moving to the app

DharmaAI is becoming part of the DharmaChat mobile app, where it can sit alongside your daily sadhana, your shlokas and your practice. Leave your email and we will tell you the day it opens.

What is changing

DharmaAI answers real questions from scripture: what karma yoga means in an ordinary job, why Arjuna hesitated, what the Gita says about grief, anger or doubt. That guidance is now being built into the app rather than the website.

Why: in the app, DharmaAI knows where you are in your practice. It can point you to the exact shloka you were reading, hold the thread of a conversation, and speak with you in English, Hindi or Kannada. A browser tab cannot do any of that.

  • 173 shlokas with word by word meaning, translation and commentary
  • DharmaAI, rooted in the Gita, Ramayana, Mahabharata and Upanishads
  • Daily sadhana, karma points, streaks and a Hindu festival calendar
  • Dharma Sabha, a moderated prayer wall where seekers hold each other in prayer

Be told the day it launches

One email, when the app is live. Nothing else.

In the meantime, everything else on the site is open and free to read.
